Hi! This is Videoner — a free service that downloads videos from social media. You can use it in two ways: on the website and in Telegram.

## What it can do

- Downloads videos from **YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Twitter/X**.
- Shows the available qualities — you choose the one you need.
- Can give you just the audio if you don't need the video.

## How to download on the website

1. Copy the video link.
2. Open [videoner.download](https://videoner.download) and paste the link into the field.
3. Choose a quality — the file is saved to your "Downloads" folder.

The website is available in Russian, English and Spanish — switch the language in the menu.

## How to download in Telegram

1. Open [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) and tap "Start".
2. Send the bot a video link.
3. Tap a quality button — the bot sends the file right to the chat.

The bot replies in Russian or English, depending on your Telegram language.

It's handy when you need to save a video from your phone: no need to hunt for a downloader in the browser — just share the link with the bot.
