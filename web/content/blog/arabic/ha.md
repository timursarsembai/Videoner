Yanzu Videoner yana samuwa da **Larabci** — harshe na shida bayan Turanci, Sifaniyanci, Rashanci, Yukrainanci da Kazakh.

## Abin da aka fassara

- **Shafin yanar gizo** — kowane shafi, rubuce-rubucen blog da takardun doka. Sigar Larabci tana a [videoner.download/ar](https://videoner.download/ar), kuma an tsara shafin daga dama zuwa hagu, kamar yadda masu karatun Larabci ke tsammani.
- **Bot** — dukan saƙonni, maɓallai da menu na umarni. Idan an saita Telegram ɗinku da Larabci, bot zai amsa da Larabci da kansa. Za ku iya canza harshe da `/language`.
- **Ƙarin burauza** — tagar ƙarin, maɓallin Sauke a shafukan bidiyo da saƙonnin kuskure. Ku zaɓi harshe a tagar ƙarin.

## Yadda za ku zaɓi harshe

- A shafin yanar gizo — mai sauya harshe a menu na sama.
- A cikin bot — `/language`; a rukuni — `/settings`.
- A cikin ƙarin burauza — jerin harsuna a tagarsa.
