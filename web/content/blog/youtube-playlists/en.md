Two big changes.

## No more daily limit

You used to be able to download no more than 20 files a day. Now that limit is gone in both the bot and on the site — download as much as you need, free, just like before.

## YouTube playlists

Send a playlist link and Videoner will download every video in it, one at a time, in order. You can choose:

- **720p** — good quality and small files;
- **best quality** — everything the video has;
- **audio only (mp3)** — handy for music, podcasts and lectures.

You can stop the download at any moment with the **Stop** button — both the current video and all the following ones will stop.

## In the bot

Send [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) a playlist link and choose the quality. The bot will send the videos one by one. To stop, use the button under the message or the **/stop** command.

If you send a link to a video from a playlist, the bot will download that video and separately offer to download the whole playlist. Flags work too: `playlist link -audio` — the whole playlist in mp3 right away.

## On the site and in the extension

On [videoner.download](https://videoner.download), paste a playlist link — a playlist panel with quality options will appear. Files are saved to Downloads one at a time. Don't close the page while the download is running; the browser may ask once for permission to download multiple files.

In the [browser extension](https://videoner.download/extension), open a playlist or a video from it and click the Videoner icon — there will be a playlist block at the bottom of the popup. You can close the popup: the download will continue.

Playlists can't be downloaded in groups, so the chat doesn't get flooded with dozens of videos.
