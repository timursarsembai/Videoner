People often forward a video for the author's caption: a recipe, an address, a joke, a list of hashtags. Now [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) keeps it together with the file.

## What changed in the caption

The video title is now followed by the **post text**, exactly as the author wrote it. This works for posts from social networks:

- TikTok;
- Instagram;
- Threads;
- X (Twitter);
- Facebook;
- Pinterest.

On YouTube, VK, Rutube and other video hosting sites, the description usually consists of links and timestamps, so the bot doesn't add it.

If the post title is just the beginning of its text, the bot doesn't repeat it twice. Long text is trimmed: a Telegram caption can't be longer than 1024 characters, and the source link must stay intact.

## In groups

The post text goes into the full caption. If a group admin chose a short caption ("title and source") or no caption in /settings, the bot sends videos the same way as before.

## News channel on the site and in the extension

After a download on [videoner.download](https://videoner.download) and in the [browser extension](https://videoner.download/extension), there is now a link to our channel [@videonerdownload](https://t.me/videonerdownload). We post about every update there.
