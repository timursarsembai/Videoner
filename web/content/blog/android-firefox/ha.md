Domin bot ya sauke stories da wallafe-wallafe na sirri na Instagram, dole ku haɗa asusunku da shi. Har zuwa yanzu, wannan yana buƙatar kwamfuta mai ƙarin burauza na Videoner. Yanzu za ku iya haɗawa kai tsaye daga wayarku ta **Android**.

## Abin da kuke buƙata

- Wayar Android.
- Burauzar **Firefox** daga Google Play: Chrome na waya ba ya tallafa wa ƙari, amma Firefox yana tallafawa.
- Ƙarin **Cookie-Editor** na Firefox.

## Yadda ake haɗawa

1. Ku sanya Firefox kuma ku shiga asusunku a instagram.com a cikinsa.
2. Menu na Firefox (⋮) → “Add-ons” → ku nemo kuma ku sanya **Cookie-Editor**.
3. Ku buɗe instagram.com, sannan menu (⋮) → “Add-ons” → Cookie-Editor.
4. Ku taɓa **Export** kuma ku zaɓi tsarin **Netscape** — za a kwafi bayanan shigarku zuwa allon kwafi.
5. Ku buɗe [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) a hira ta sirri kuma ku liƙa rubutun da aka kwafa a matsayin saƙo na yau da kullum.

Bot zai amsa cewa an haɗa asusun. Yanzu ku aika masa hanyoyin haɗi zuwa stories — zai sauke su ta amfani da shigarku. Za ku iya duba haɗin kuma ku cire shi da umarnin **/accounts**.

## Muhimmi

- Wannan rubutu yana ba da damar shiga asusunku na Instagram. Ku aika shi **ga bot kawai a hira ta sirri** — ba a rukuni ba, kuma ba ga wasu mutane ba.
- Bot yana adana bayanan shiga na dandalin da yake buƙata kawai, kuma yana adana su a ɓoye (encrypted).
- Dandamali ba sa son saukewa ta atomatik kuma suna iya taƙaita asusunku, don haka gara ku haɗa asusu na biyu maimakon babban asusunku.
- Wannan yana haɗa asusu ne don ku kaɗai. Don haɗa asusu don dukan rukuni, ku yi amfani da [ƙarin burauza a kwamfutarku](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) da umarnin /connect.

## Me game da iPhone?

Wannan ba zai yi aiki a iPhone ba: Safari ba ya barin a fitar da bayanan shiga. A can, ya fi sauƙi ku haɗa asusunku sau ɗaya daga kwamfuta ta amfani da ƙarin burauza na Videoner.
