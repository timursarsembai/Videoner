Yanzu Videoner yana samuwa da sababbin harsuna biyu — **Yukrainanci** da **Kazakh**.

## Inda sababbin harsunan suke

- **Bot** [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) — dukan saƙonni, maɓallai da menu na umarni.
- **Shafin yanar gizo** — sigogin Yukrainanci da Kazakh na [videoner.download](https://videoner.download/ha), ana sauya su a menu na harshe.
- **Ƙarin burauza** — fuskar tagar ƙarin da maɓallin a shafukan bidiyo.

Idan an saita Telegram ko burauzarku da Yukrainanci ko Kazakh, Videoner zai amsa da wannan harshe ta atomatik.

## Zaɓar harshe da hannu

A da, bot yana bin harshen Telegram ɗinku. Yanzu za ku iya zaɓar harshe da kanku:

1. Ku aika wa bot umarnin **/language**.
2. Ku zaɓi harshe: Русский, Українська, Қазақша ko English.
3. Don komawa ga tsoho, ku zaɓi “Atomatik — kamar a Telegram”.

## Harshe a rukuni

Masu gudanar da rukuni za su iya saita harshen amsar bot: **/settings → “Harshe”**. A tsoho, bot a rukuni yana amsa kowane memba da harshen Telegram ɗinsa.

## Harshe a ƙarin burauza

Yanzu tagar ƙarin tana da zaɓin harshe a kanta. Maɓallan da ke shafukan bidiyo ma suna sauyawa zuwa harshen da aka zaɓa. Ku sabunta ƙarin a [shafin ƙarin](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) idan kuna amfani da Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge ko Opera — Firefox yana sabuntawa da kansa.
