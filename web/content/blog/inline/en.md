Two new ways to download videos faster.

## Videos right in the conversation

Want to show a friend a video without just forwarding the link? You can now send a video to any Telegram chat without leaving it:

1. In the message field, type `@VideonerBot`, a space and the video link.
2. A "Download video" card appears above the keyboard — tap it.
3. A "Downloading, this may take a couple of minutes..." message appears in the chat, and a couple of minutes later the video itself takes its place.

You don't need to add the bot to the chat — this works in any conversation.

A couple of things to note:

- There's no way to choose the quality here, so the video arrives in **720p**.
- Inline mode sends a single file. For carousels and posts with photos, open [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) and send the link there.

## A shortcut on the website

[videoner.download](https://videoner.download) now has a shortcut. Add `videoner.download/` in front of a video link in your browser's address bar, for example:

`videoner.download/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`

The page for the right platform opens with the link already pasted in and the qualities loading straight away. It's handy: you just put the cursor at the start of the address, with nothing to select or copy.

It works for every platform Videoner supports.
