We've started a blog on the website and a news channel on Telegram. This is where we'll briefly cover what's new in the bot, on the website and in the extension, and how to use it.

## Where to read

- The blog is on the website, under "Blog" in the menu: [videoner.download](https://videoner.download).
- The [@videonerdownload](https://t.me/videonerdownload) channel has the same articles right in Telegram.

You don't have to subscribe to the channel: the bot works with no strings attached, just as before. The channel is simply a handy way to be the first to hear about new features.

## Link in the caption

The caption of every file sent by [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) now includes a line with a link to the news channel. Everything else in the caption stays the same: the title, the source and the "Share" line.

## Catching up on past updates

To keep the project's history in one place, we've added posts to the blog about the major updates since the launch in summer 2026: new platforms, albums and Threads, inline mode, the browser extension, group mode, Instagram stories and new languages. Each article is dated the day the feature actually shipped.
