Kordhiyaha biraawsarka Videoner wuxuu helay laba wax cusub: taariikhda soo dejinta, iyo rakibidda Firefox oo laga sameeyo katalogga rasmiga ah ee Mozilla.

## Taariikhda soo dejinta

Daaqadda kordhiyaha, meel u dhow doorashada luqadda, waxaa hadda ku jira badhan saacad leh. Wuxuu furaa liiska wax kasta oo aad kordhiyaha ku soo dejisay **30-kii maalmood** ee la soo dhaafay:

- **magaca** muuqaalka — guji si aad mar kale u furto boggiisa;
- **taariikhda iyo waqtiga** soo dejinta;
- calaamadda **MP3** haddii aad soo dejisay cod oo keliya.

Haddii aad mar kale soo dejiso isla muuqaalka, qoraalkiisu wuxuu si fudud ugu dul baxayaa liiska. Badhanka “Tirtir taariikhda” wuxuu tirtiraa liiska oo dhan.

Taariikhda waxaa lagu kaydiyaa **biraawsarkaaga oo keliya**: looma diro server-ka Videoner, qof aan adiga ahaynna ma arki karo.

## Firefox: katalogga add-on-yada Mozilla

Videoner hadda wuxuu ku jiraa [katalogga add-on-yada Mozilla](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/videoner-video-downloader/). Badhanka Firefox ee [bogga kordhiyaha](https://videoner.download/so/extension) halkaas ayuu ku geynayaa — riix “Add to Firefox”, waa intaas. Cusbooneysiinta Firefox ayaa iskiis u rakiba.

Haddii aad horey kordhiyaha uga rakibtay websaydhkayaga, waxba ma aad samaynaysid: marka xigta ee Firefox hubiyo cusbooneysiinta, iskiis ayuu ugu wareegayaa nooca katalogga, isagoo haya dhammaan dejintaada.

## Sida loo cusbooneysiiyo

- **Firefox** — iskiis ayuu u cusbooneysiiyaa muddo maalin gudaheed ah.
- **Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge, Opera** — astaanta Videoner waxaa ka soo muuqanaysa calaamadda NEW. Ka soo deji kaydka (archive) cusub [bogga kordhiyaha](https://videoner.download/so/extension#update) oo ku fur isla galkii hore, adigoo beddelaya faylasha.
