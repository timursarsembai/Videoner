Ƙarin burauza na Videoner ya samu sababbin abubuwa biyu: tarihin saukewa, da sanya shi a Firefox daga kundin hukuma na Mozilla.

## Tarihin saukewa

A tagar ƙarin, kusa da zaɓin harshe, yanzu akwai maɓallin agogo. Yana buɗe jerin duk abin da kuka sauke ta ƙarin cikin **kwanaki 30** da suka wuce:

- **sunan** bidiyon — ku danna shi don sake buɗe shafin bidiyon;
- **kwanan wata da lokacin** saukewa;
- alamar **MP3** idan sauti kawai kuka sauke.

Idan kuka sake sauke bidiyon ɗaya, shigarsa kawai za ta hau sama. Maɓallin “Share tarihi” yana goge dukan jerin.

Ana ajiye tarihin **a burauzarku kaɗai**: ba a taɓa aika shi zuwa sabar Videoner ba, kuma babu wanda zai iya ganinsa sai ku.

## Firefox: kundin add-on na Mozilla

Yanzu Videoner yana cikin [kundin add-on na Mozilla](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/videoner-video-downloader/). Maɓallin Firefox a [shafin ƙarin](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) yana kai ku can — ku danna “Add to Firefox”, shi ke nan. Firefox yana sanya sabuntawa da kansa.

Idan kun riga kun sanya ƙarin daga shafinmu, ba sai kun yi komai ba: a binciken sabuntawa na gaba, Firefox zai koma da kansa zuwa sigar kundin, tare da dukan saitunanku.

## Yadda ake sabuntawa

- **Firefox** — yana sabuntawa da kansa cikin kwana ɗaya.
- **Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge, Opera** — alamar NEW za ta bayyana a gunkin Videoner. Ku sauke sabuwar ma'ajiyar (archive) a [shafin ƙarin](https://videoner.download/ha/extension#update) ku buɗe ta a cikin babban fayil ɗin da yake, ku maye gurbin fayilolin.
