Videoner is now available in **25 languages**. English, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Kazakh and Arabic are joined by 19 more:

- **Asian:** Chinese (Simplified) — [/zh](https://videoner.download/zh), Hindi — [/hi](https://videoner.download/hi), Bengali — [/bn](https://videoner.download/bn), Urdu — [/ur](https://videoner.download/ur), Japanese — [/ja](https://videoner.download/ja), Korean — [/ko](https://videoner.download/ko), Thai — [/th](https://videoner.download/th), Vietnamese — [/vi](https://videoner.download/vi), Indonesian — [/id](https://videoner.download/id), Tagalog — [/tl](https://videoner.download/tl)
- **Central Asia and the Middle East:** Uzbek — [/uz](https://videoner.download/uz), Turkish — [/tr](https://videoner.download/tr), Persian — [/fa](https://videoner.download/fa)
- **European:** German — [/de](https://videoner.download/de), French — [/fr](https://videoner.download/fr), Italian — [/it](https://videoner.download/it), Portuguese (Brazilian) — [/pt](https://videoner.download/pt), Polish — [/pl](https://videoner.download/pl)
- **African:** Swahili — [/sw](https://videoner.download/sw)

Spanish used to be on the website only — now the bot and the extension speak it too.

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents. Arabic, Persian and Urdu are shown right to left.
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to one of these languages, the bot answers in it on its own.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.

All three now list the languages in the same order: by each language's own name.
