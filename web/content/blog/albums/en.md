Until now, you could download only one video from a post with several files. Now Videoner grabs the whole post.

## Carousels and photos

- **Every file in the post** is downloaded: several videos, several photos, or a mix of both.
- **Photos** are saved as regular JPEGs in the best available size.
- If a post contains only photos, there's no quality to choose — you get a single button with the original.

**In the bot**, a carousel arrives as an album. Telegram allows no more than 10 files per album, so a large post comes as several albums. A single-photo post is sent as a photo, not as a video.

**On the website**, each file in the post is shown separately with its own button, and the "Download all" button saves them one after another.

## Threads

We've added **Threads**: send a link to a post, and Videoner will download the videos and photos from it. Short links from the "Share" button in the Threads mobile app work too.

## File captions

The bot now adds a caption to the files it sends: the title and a link to the original. That makes it easy to tell where a video came from in your chat.

## How to try it

Open [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) and send a link to any Instagram carousel or Threads post. Or paste it on [videoner.download](https://videoner.download).
