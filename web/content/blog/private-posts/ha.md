Wasu wallafe-wallafe ana nuna su ne kawai ga mutanen da suka shiga asusunsu: stories na Instagram, wallafe-wallafe da reels na sirri, wallafe-wallafe na abokai kawai, bidiyo masu iyakar shekaru. Har zuwa yanzu bot yana amsa irin waɗannan hanyoyin haɗi da “ba ya samuwa ba tare da shiga ba” kawai.

## Me ya sabunta

Bot yana bayyana abin da ke faruwa kuma yana ba ku maɓallin **“🔑 Haɗa asusu”**. Kuna haɗa asusu sau ɗaya — bayan haka bot yana sauke duk abin da kuke iya gani kai tsaye a Telegram, har ma daga wayarku.

Idan an riga an haɗa asusun amma wallafar ba ta buɗe ba duk da haka, bot yana ba da shawarar **sake haɗa shi**: watakila shigar ta daina aiki, ko asusun ba shi da damar ganin wannan wallafa.

Don Instagram, Facebook da TikTok akwai kuma maɓalli na biyu — sauke wallafar da [ƙarin burauza](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) a kwamfuta.

## Yadda ake haɗa asusu

- **A kwamfuta** — maɓallin “🔑 Haɗa asusunku na … da bot” a tagar ƙarin Videoner.
- **A wayar Android** — da Firefox da ƙarin Cookie-Editor, [umarni](https://videoner.download/ha/blog/android-firefox).
- **Da fayil** — ku aika cookies.txt ga bot.

Haɗe-haɗenku da cire su — umarnin `/accounts`. Gara ku haɗa wani asusu daban, ba babban asusunku ba: dandamali ba sa son saukewa ta atomatik.
