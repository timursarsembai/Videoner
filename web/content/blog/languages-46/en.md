Videoner is now available in **46 languages**. 11 more have joined:

- **Afghanistan and Kurdistan:** Pashto — [/ps](https://videoner.download/ps), Kurdish (Sorani) — [/ckb](https://videoner.download/ckb), Kurdish (Kurmanji) — [/ku](https://videoner.download/ku)
- **Central Asia and the Volga region:** Uyghur — [/ug](https://videoner.download/ug), Turkmen — [/tk](https://videoner.download/tk), Tatar — [/tt](https://videoner.download/tt)
- **The Balkans:** Albanian — [/sq](https://videoner.download/sq), Bosnian — [/bs](https://videoner.download/bs)
- **The Caucasus:** Chechen — [/ce](https://videoner.download/ce), Ingush — [/inh](https://videoner.download/inh), Avar — [/av](https://videoner.download/av)

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents. Pashto, Sorani and Uyghur are shown right to left.
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to one of these languages, the bot answers in it on its own.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.

The languages are listed in the same order everywhere: by each language's own name.
