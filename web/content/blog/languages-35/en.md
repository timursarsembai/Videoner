Videoner is now available in **35 languages**. 10 more have joined:

- **African:** Amharic — [/am](https://videoner.download/am), Oromo — [/om](https://videoner.download/om), Hausa — [/ha](https://videoner.download/ha), Somali — [/so](https://videoner.download/so)
- **Central Asia and the Caucasus:** Azerbaijani — [/az](https://videoner.download/az), Kyrgyz — [/ky](https://videoner.download/ky), Tajik — [/tg](https://videoner.download/tg)
- **Southeast Asia:** Malay — [/ms](https://videoner.download/ms)
- **European:** Dutch — [/nl](https://videoner.download/nl), Romanian — [/ro](https://videoner.download/ro)

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents.
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to one of these languages, the bot answers in it on its own.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.

The languages are listed in the same order everywhere: by each language's own name.
