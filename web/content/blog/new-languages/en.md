Videoner is now available in **14 languages**. English, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Kazakh and Arabic are joined by eight more:

- **Chinese** (Simplified) — [videoner.download/zh](https://videoner.download/zh)
- **Hindi** — [videoner.download/hi](https://videoner.download/hi)
- **Indonesian** — [videoner.download/id](https://videoner.download/id)
- **Swahili** — [videoner.download/sw](https://videoner.download/sw)
- **German** — [videoner.download/de](https://videoner.download/de)
- **French** — [videoner.download/fr](https://videoner.download/fr)
- **Italian** — [videoner.download/it](https://videoner.download/it)
- **Portuguese** (Brazilian) — [videoner.download/pt](https://videoner.download/pt)

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents.
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to one of these languages, the bot answers in it on its own.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.
