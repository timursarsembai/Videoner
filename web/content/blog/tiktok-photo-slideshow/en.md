Two updates for posts that contain several photos instead of a video.

## TikTok photo posts

TikTok has posts where you swipe through pictures rather than watch a video. Videoner used to reply to such links with an error. Now it downloads them: send a link to a photo post — either a regular one or a short one from the "Share" button.

## Photo slideshows

For any post with several photos — on TikTok, Instagram or Threads — there are now three options:

- **📷 Download photos** — all photos as an album, as before.
- **🎞 Slideshow (video)** — a single video made from all the photos: each one is shown for 3 seconds, in a vertical frame, just like on TikTok.
- **📷+🎞 Photos and slideshow** — in one album: the video first, then all the photos.

The slideshow is made without music — pictures only.

The choice is available in the [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) bot, on [videoner.download](https://videoner.download) and in the [browser extension](https://videoner.download/extension).

## In groups

There are no buttons in groups, so the bot immediately sends **photos and a slideshow** in one album. If the post has just one photo, it sends only that photo.
