Videoner is now available in two new languages — **Ukrainian** and **Kazakh**.

## Where the new languages are available

- **The bot** [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) — all messages, buttons and the command menu.
- **The website** — Ukrainian and Kazakh versions of [videoner.download](https://videoner.download), switchable in the language menu.
- **The browser extension** — the popup interface and the button on video pages.

If your Telegram or browser is set to Ukrainian or Kazakh, Videoner will reply in that language automatically.

## Choosing the language manually

Previously, the bot followed your Telegram language. Now you can choose the language yourself:

1. Send the bot the **/language** command.
2. Choose a language: Русский, Українська, Қазақша or English.
3. To go back to the default, choose "Auto — as in Telegram".

## Language in groups

Group admins can set the bot's reply language: **/settings → "Language"**. By default, the bot in a group replies to each member in their own Telegram language.

## Language in the extension

The extension popup now has a language picker in its header. The buttons on video pages switch to the chosen language too. Update the extension on the [extension page](https://videoner.download/extension) if you use Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge or Opera — Firefox updates by itself.
