The Videoner browser extension gets two new things: a download history, and installing in Firefox from Mozilla's official catalog.

## Download history

In the extension window, next to the language picker, there is now a clock button. It opens a list of everything you downloaded with the extension in the last **30 days**:

- the video **title** — click it to open the video page again;
- the **date and time** of the download;
- an **MP3** mark if you downloaded audio only.

Download the same video again and its entry simply moves to the top. The "Clear history" button deletes the whole list.

The history is stored **only in your browser**: it is never sent to the Videoner server, and nobody but you can see it.

## Firefox: the Mozilla Add-ons catalog

Videoner is now in the [Mozilla Add-ons catalog](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/videoner-video-downloader/). The Firefox button on the [extension page](https://videoner.download/extension) leads there — click "Add to Firefox" and you're done. Firefox installs updates by itself.

If you already installed the extension from our website, there's nothing to do: on its next update check Firefox switches to the catalog version by itself, keeping all your settings.

## How to update

- **Firefox** — updates by itself within a day.
- **Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge, Opera** — the Videoner icon shows a NEW mark. Download the new archive on the [extension page](https://videoner.download/extension#update) and unpack it into the same folder, replacing the files.
