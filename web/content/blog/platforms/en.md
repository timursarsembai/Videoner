We've added five new platforms. Videoner now downloads videos from:

- **YouTube**, **TikTok**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **Twitter/X** — as before;
- **Vimeo** — new;
- **VK** (VKontakte) — new;
- **Rutube** — new;
- **OK.ru** (Odnoklassniki) — new;
- **Pinterest** — new: videos from pins.

## How to use it

There's nothing to set up. Copy the video link and paste it on [videoner.download](https://videoner.download) or send it to the [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) bot. Videoner detects the platform on its own and shows the available qualities.

If a Pinterest pin has no video, the bot and the website will tell you so plainly instead of failing with a cryptic error.

## A dedicated page for each platform

Each platform now has its own page on the website — you'll find it in the site menu. It includes a short guide on how to copy a link in that particular app and answers to frequently asked questions. The pages are available in Russian, English and Spanish.

Message the bot if the platform you need isn't on the list yet — that helps us decide what to add next. News is posted in the [@videonerdownload](https://t.me/videonerdownload) channel.
