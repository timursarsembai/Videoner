Manyan sababbin abubuwa biyu a ƙarin Videoner.

## Darussan GetCourse

Yanzu ƙarin yana sauke bidiyon darussa daga makarantun kan layi da ke kan GetCourse.

1. Ku shiga makarantarku kuma ku buɗe darasi.
2. Maɓallin **“Sauke”** yana bayyana a kusurwar sama ta dama ta na'urar kunna bidiyo.
3. Idan darasin yana da bidiyo da yawa, ku danna alamar Videoner a sandar kayan aiki — za ta jera su duka.

Burauzarku ce take sauke darasin da kanta kuma ta haɗa shi zuwa **mp4** na yau da kullum. Fayil ba ya wucewa ta sabar mu, don haka za ku iya sauke duk abin da kuke iya gani yayin da kuka shiga makarantarku. Idan saukewa a burauza ta gaza, sabar Videoner za ta sauke wannan darasi a irin ingancin.

Ku lura: ana haɗa darasin a ƙwaƙwalwar burauza kuma ana adana shi gaba ɗaya a ƙarshe, don haka dogon bidiyo mai inganci sosai zai ɗauki ɗan lokaci.

## Sigar Firefox

Yanzu ƙarin yana samuwa don **Mozilla Firefox 128 zuwa sama**. Yana yin duk abin da ɗayan sigar take yi: maɓallin “Sauke” a shafin bidiyo, saukewa da shigarku, da darussan GetCourse.

Sanyawa da danna ɗaya:

1. Ku buɗe [shafin ƙarin](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) a Firefox ku danna maɓallin Firefox.
2. Firefox zai tambaya ko zai yarda da sanyawa daga videoner.download — ku danna “Continue to Installation”.
3. A cikin tambayar “Add Videoner?”, ku danna “Add”.

Mozilla ta sanya hannu a kan ƙarin, kuma Firefox yana sanya sabuntawa da kansa.

Yanzu shafin ƙarin yana da maɓallai biyu — ɗaya don burauzoji irin na Chrome, ɗaya kuma don Firefox — kuma an haskaka burauzarku.
