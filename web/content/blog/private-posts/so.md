Boostooyinka qaarkood waxaa la tusaa oo keliya dadka akoonkooda ku jira: stories-ka Instagram, boostooyinka iyo reels-ka gaarka ah, boostooyinka saaxiibbada oo keliya, muuqaallada da'da lagu xaddiday. Ilaa hadda bot-ku link-yadaas wuxuu ugu jawaabi jiray oo keliya “lama heli karo iyadoon la gelin”.

## Waxa cusub

Bot-ku wuxuu sharxaa waxa dhacaya wuxuuna ku siinayaa badhan **“🔑 Ku xir akoonka”**. Akoon hal mar ayaad ku xiraysaa — kadib bot-ku wuxuu si toos ah Telegram ugu soo dejiyaa wax kasta oo aad arki karto, xitaa taleefankaaga.

Haddii akoonku horay u xirnaa laakiin boostadu weli aysan furmin, bot-ku wuxuu soo jeediyaa in **mar kale la xiro**: galitaanku waxaa laga yaabaa inuu joogsaday, ama akoonku ma laha gelitaanka boostadan.

Instagram, Facebook iyo TikTok waxaa sidoo kale jira badhan labaad — ku soo deji boostada [kordhiyaha biraawsarka](https://videoner.download/so/extension) ee kombiyuutarka.

## Sida akoon loo xiro

- **Kombiyuutarka** — badhanka “🔑 Ku xir akoonkaaga … bot-ka” ee daaqadda kordhiyaha Videoner.
- **Taleefan Android ah** — Firefox iyo add-on-ka Cookie-Editor, [tilmaamaha](https://videoner.download/so/blog/android-firefox).
- **Fayl** — u soo dir bot-ka cookies.txt.

Xiriiradaada iyo ka saaristooda — amarka `/accounts`. Waxaa fiican in aad ku xirto akoon gaar ah oo aan ahayn kaaga ugu muhiimsan: madalladu ma jecla soo dejinta tooska ah.
