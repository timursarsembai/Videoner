Here's a roundup of the latest updates to the [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) bot.

## Up to 4K and files up to 2 GB

The bot gives you everything the platform has, all the way up to **4K**. The only limit is file size: Telegram lets bots send files up to **2 GB**. Qualities that don't fit within that limit simply aren't shown, so you won't wait for a download that would never arrive.

## Sizes on the buttons

Every quality button now shows the **approximate file size**. You can see at a glance how much a video weighs in 1080p versus 720p and pick what suits your connection and your phone's storage.

## Download progress

Instead of "wait a couple of minutes", the bot shows how things are going: **percentage, time remaining and how much has been downloaded**. The message updates every few seconds, and when the file is being put together, the bot tells you it's almost done.

## Link queue

You can send **several links at once** — in one message or one after another. The bot will queue them:

- you choose the quality for one link at a time;
- the next link shows up once the bot has sent the file for the previous one;
- you can skip a link you don't need with a button;
- the queue holds up to 10 links, and duplicates aren't added.

And while one person is downloading a long video, the bot no longer "freezes" for everyone else.

## YouTube subtitles

YouTube videos now have a **"Subtitles"** button. Tap it, pick a language, and the bot will send you an **.srt** file. These are the video author's subtitles, or auto-generated ones if there are none in the video's language. Afterwards you can go back and download the video itself.

The same goes for [videoner.download](https://videoner.download): next to "Video" and "Audio" there's a "Subtitles" button.
