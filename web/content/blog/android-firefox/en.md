For the bot to download Instagram stories and private posts, you need to connect your account to it. Until now, this required a computer with the Videoner extension. Now you can connect right from your **Android** phone.

## What you need

- An Android phone.
- The **Firefox** browser from Google Play: mobile Chrome doesn't support add-ons, but Firefox does.
- The **Cookie-Editor** add-on for Firefox.

## How to connect

1. Install Firefox and log in to your account on instagram.com in it.
2. Firefox menu (⋮) → "Add-ons" → find and install **Cookie-Editor**.
3. Open instagram.com, then menu (⋮) → "Add-ons" → Cookie-Editor.
4. Tap **Export** and choose the **Netscape** format — your login data will be copied to the clipboard.
5. Open [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) in a private chat and paste the copied text as a regular message.

The bot will reply that the account is connected. Now send it links to stories — it will download them using your login. You can view and disconnect the connection with the **/accounts** command.

## Important

- This text gives access to your Instagram account. Send it **only to the bot in a private chat** — not to a group and not to other people.
- The bot keeps only the login data for the platform it needs and stores it encrypted.
- Platforms don't like automated downloads and may restrict your account, so it's better to connect a secondary one rather than your main account.
- This connects an account for you personally. To connect an account for a whole group, use the [extension on your computer](https://videoner.download/extension) and the /connect command.

## What about iPhone?

This won't work on iPhone: Safari doesn't let you export login data. There, it's easier to connect your account once from a computer using the Videoner extension.
