Stories da wallafe-wallafe na sirri suna bayyana ne kawai ga waɗanda suka shiga asusunsu, don haka sabar Videoner ba za ta iya samun su ba. Yanzu akwai mafita biyu don haka.

## Stories a ƙarin burauza

[Ƙarin Videoner](https://videoner.download/ha/extension) yana sauke **stories na Instagram** kai tsaye a burauza, ta amfani da shigarku. Ku buɗe story ku danna “Sauke”: ana adana bidiyo a matsayin mp4, hotuna kuma a matsayin jpg.

## Haɗa asusu da bot

Domin bot ma ya iya sauke stories da wallafe-wallafe na sirri, ku haɗa asusunku da shi:

1. Ku shiga shafin dandalin a burauza mai ƙarin Videoner.
2. Ku danna alamar Videoner, sannan a ƙasan tagar, **“🔑 Haɗa asusunku na … da bot”**.
3. Ku ba da izini lokacin da burauza ta tambaya — ƙarin zai nuna lamba (code).
4. Ku aika lambar ga [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) a hira ta sirri. Lambar tana aiki na minti 10.

Za ku iya haɗa **Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X, VK da TikTok**. Babu buƙatar kalmar sirri: bayanan shiga na wannan dandali kawai ke zuwa sabar, a ɓoye (encrypted).

Bayan haka, bot yana sauke stories da wallafe-wallafe na sirri daga hanyoyin haɗinku. Idan kuka aika hanyar haɗi zuwa bayanan martaba (profile) mai stories, bot zai aika stories na yanzu a matsayin kundi; idan hakan bai yi aiki ba, zai nemi hanyar haɗi zuwa wani takamaiman story.

Shawara: dandamali ba sa son saukewa ta atomatik kuma suna iya taƙaita asusu — gara ku haɗa asusu na biyu, ba babban asusunku ba.

## Don rukuni

Domin bot ya sauke stories don dukan rukuni, ku aika **/connect CODE** a wannan rukunin. Wanda zai iya haɗa asusu — masu gudanarwa kawai ko kowa — ana saita shi a /settings. Idan ba a haɗa asusu don rukunin ba, bot yana sauke hanyar haɗinku ta amfani da haɗinku na kanku.

## Sarrafa haɗe-haɗe

- **/accounts** — jerin haɗe-haɗenku: za ku iya cire ɗaya ko duka a lokaci ɗaya.
- Maɓallin **“Haɗe-haɗena a bot”** a ƙarin burauza yana buɗe wannan jerin.
- Maɓallin **“Janye izinin cookies”** a ƙarin burauza yana cire izinin — ƙarin ba ya sake karanta bayanan shiga. Asusun da kuka riga kuka haɗa suna nan a bot; za ku iya cire su ta /accounts.
