Videoner is now available in **Arabic** — the sixth language after English, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian and Kazakh.

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents. The Arabic version lives at [videoner.download/ar](https://videoner.download/ar), and the page is laid out right to left, the way Arabic readers expect.
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to Arabic, the bot answers in Arabic on its own. You can switch the language with `/language`.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages. Pick the language in the extension window.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.
