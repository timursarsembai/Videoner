Videoner hadda waxaa lagu heli karaa laba luqadood oo cusub — **Yukreeniyaan** iyo **Kazakh**.

## Meesha luqadaha cusub laga heli karo

- **Bot-ka** [@VideonerBot](https://t.me/VideonerBot) — dhammaan fariimaha, badhamada iyo menu-ga amarrada.
- **Websaydhka** — nuqullada Yukreeniyaan iyo Kazakh ee [videoner.download](https://videoner.download/so), oo lagu beddelo menu-ga luqadda.
- **Kordhiyaha biraawsarka** — muuqaalka daaqadda iyo badhanka bogagga muuqaallada.

Haddii Telegram-kaaga ama biraawsarkaagu ku dejisan yahay Yukreeniyaan ama Kazakh, Videoner si toos ah ayuu luqaddaas kuugu jawaabi doonaa.

## Doorashada luqadda gacanta

Hore, bot-ku wuxuu raacayay luqadda Telegram-kaaga. Hadda luqadda adigaa iskaa u dooran kara:

1. U dir bot-ka amarka **/language**.
2. Dooro luqad: Русский, Українська, Қазақша ama English.
3. Si aad ugu noqoto caadiga, dooro “Otomaatig — sida Telegram”.

## Luqadda kooxaha

Maamulayaasha kooxdu waxay dejin karaan luqadda jawaabaha bot-ka: **/settings → “Luqadda”**. Sida caadiga ah, bot-ku kooxda dhexdeeda wuxuu xubin kasta ugu jawaabaa luqadda Telegram-kiisa.

## Luqadda kordhiyaha

Daaqadda kordhiyaha hadda waxay madaxeeda ku leedahay doorashada luqadda. Badhamada bogagga muuqaallada sidoo kale waxay u beddelmaan luqadda la doortay. Ka cusbooneysii kordhiyaha [bogga kordhiyaha](https://videoner.download/so/extension) haddii aad isticmaasho Chrome, Yandex Browser, Edge ama Opera — Firefox iskiis ayuu isu cusbooneysiiyaa.
