title: Videoner now speaks Chinese
summary: The website, the bot and the browser extension are now available in Simplified Chinese.
date: 2026-10-31
---
Videoner is now available in **Chinese** (Simplified) — the seventh language after English, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Kazakh and Arabic.

## What is translated

- **The website** — every page, the blog articles and the legal documents. The Chinese version: [videoner.download/zh](https://videoner.download/zh).
- **The bot** — all messages, buttons and the command menu. If your Telegram is set to Chinese, the bot answers in Chinese on its own. You can switch the language with `/language`.
- **The browser extension** — the extension window, the Download button on video pages and the error messages.

## How to choose the language

- On the website — the language switcher in the top menu.
- In the bot — `/language`; in a group — `/settings`.
- In the extension — the language list in its window.
